PLATE 01 / A STUDY IN GREEN

From the notebook

THREE THINGS TO NOTICE
01 — LIGHT

The hour before evening

A room changes long before the sun disappears. Colours become warmer, edges soften, and the same corner feels like a different place. Sometimes the best observation is simply to stay a little longer.

02 — GROWTH

Space for a new leaf

A small plant makes time visible. One leaf unfolds while another turns toward the window. There is no need to measure every change; noticing that something has changed can be enough.

03 — WEATHER

After a little rain

Paths darken, dust settles, and the air carries the smell of soil. The garden does not become a new place. It reveals a few details that were already there, waiting for different weather.

A small practice

Return to one place.

Choose a tree, a street corner, or the view from a window. Visit it again at a different hour. Look for one thing that has changed and one thing that has stayed the same. A place becomes familiar through attention, not distance.

A note on this notebook

Nothing extraordinary required.

Field Notes is a small collection of writing about everyday surroundings. There is no itinerary and no list to complete. Just a reason to slow down, look twice, and leave a little room for curiosity.
